Privacy Policy

App: Finanze • Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 4, 2026

Welcome to Finanze (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose your information when you use our mobile application (the “App”) available on Google Play.

Summary: Finanze is a personal budgeting and task management app. We collect basic account credentials and your personal financial entries solely to provide budget tracking and sync services. We never sell your personal or financial data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide when using the App, as well as limited technical data necessary for app functionality:

  • Account Information: When you register or log in, we collect your Username and Email Address. Passwords are securely hashed before transmission and are never stored in plain text.
  • Financial Data: Any income and expense records you log, including amounts, dates, custom transaction categories, and selected currency preferences.
  • Task & Reminder Data: Task titles, due dates, reminder times, and priority levels you create to schedule alarms.
  • Device & Usage Information: Device model, operating system version, and general network connection state required to maintain session stability.
  • Advertising & Analytics Identifiers: Non-sensitive identifiers such as Google Advertising ID (GAID) collected by third-party ad networks (Google AdMob) to display advertisements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain personal budgeting, 3D chart analytics, and task tracking features.
  • To securely authenticate your account and facilitate password recovery via email.
  • To schedule local system alarms and reminder notifications on your device.
  • To synchronize your records between your local device cache and our secure cloud backend.
  • To serve banner and interstitial ads through Google AdMob to keep the App free of charge.

3. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

Your data security is a priority. We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Finanze app and our cloud backend are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Cloud authentication and database services are hosted on Appwrite Cloud, a secure, modern backend architecture.
  • Local Cache: Data is saved on your personal device using private, sandboxed local storage (SharedPreferences and internal application files), protecting it from other apps on your device.

4. Third-Party Services and SDKs

We may integrate vetted third-party services that collect information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Used to deliver advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, IP addresses, and interaction metrics to serve relevant or non-personalized ads.
    Learn more: Google Privacy & Terms
  • Appwrite Cloud: Used for secure cloud authentication and database management.
    Learn more: Appwrite Privacy Policy

5. User Rights and Data Deletion Policy

You maintain complete ownership of your personal and financial data. In accordance with Google Play policies and global privacy regulations:

  • Access and Edit: You can edit or delete individual transactions, categories, and tasks directly within the App at any time.
  • Clear Local Cache: You can clear all cached data by uninstalling the app or clearing App Storage from your Android device settings.
  • Complete Account & Cloud Data Deletion: If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated transactions, tasks, and profile records from our cloud database, you can submit a deletion request by emailing us at bhanu9885@gmail.com with the subject line “Finanze Account Deletion Request”. Your request will be verified and all associated records permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Children’s Privacy

Finanze is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will immediately delete that information from our records.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app features or applicable laws. Any modifications will be posted directly on this webpage with an updated “Last Updated” date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
